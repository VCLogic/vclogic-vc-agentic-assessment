# ShiftPilot

## One-line pitch

ShiftPilot is workforce-operations software for home-health agencies, helping them match qualified clinicians to patient visits, replace last-minute cancellations, and reduce costly scheduling gaps.

## The problem

Home-health agencies coordinate hundreds or thousands of visits every week using fragmented scheduling systems, spreadsheets, phone calls, and text messages. When a clinician becomes unavailable, an operations manager may spend hours identifying a qualified replacement who has the right credentials, availability, location, and overtime capacity.

An unfilled visit is not merely an administrative inconvenience. It can interrupt a patient's continuity of care, reduce agency revenue, create compliance risk, and increase workload for an already stretched operations team.

## The product

ShiftPilot connects with an agency's existing patient-management and scheduling systems. It creates a real-time operational view of:

- clinician credentials and qualifications;
- availability and geographic proximity;
- scheduled hours and overtime risk;
- patient requirements;
- open visits and last-minute cancellations.

When a gap appears, ShiftPilot recommends qualified clinicians and automates personalized replacement outreach. The agency's operations manager retains approval of every assignment. ShiftPilot does not deliver clinical care or make treatment decisions.

## Customer and business model

Our initial customer is a US home-health agency with approximately 100–500 clinicians. The economic buyer is usually the owner, chief operating officer, or director of operations.

Agencies currently pay an average of **$2,330 per month** for the complete workforce-operations platform. Pricing is based on agency size and operational volume rather than a per-seat model.

## Traction

We currently have:

- 8 paying agency customers;
- $18,640 in monthly recurring revenue;
- 3 additional agencies in paid implementation;
- 96% gross revenue retention over the limited period for which cohort data is available;
- an average initial sales cycle of approximately 10 weeks.

Across active customers, ShiftPilot has processed more than 41,000 visit assignments. Customers report that replacement coordination is faster, but we have not yet completed an independently verified study of labor savings or patient outcomes.

## Go-to-market

The founders currently lead sales. Initial customers came through home-health operator referrals, industry associations, and targeted outbound outreach. Implementation takes approximately three weeks because the team must map credentials, workflows, and scheduling data for each agency.

Our near-term go-to-market plan is to deepen one repeatable customer segment before expanding the sales team. We intend to focus on multi-branch agencies where missed visits and coordinator workload are especially costly.

## Market and expansion

The US contains thousands of home-health agencies, but agency size, software maturity, and willingness to pay vary substantially. Our immediate serviceable market is the segment large enough to employ dedicated operations teams yet small enough to lack internally developed workforce systems.

If we establish a trusted operational layer for staffing, the product could later support capacity planning, credential monitoring, and branch-level workforce analytics. We have not yet validated separate module pricing or demonstrated that customers will pay more than the current bundled subscription.

## Competition

Agencies can continue using their existing electronic visit verification or patient-management system, add manual coordination through spreadsheets and messaging tools, or buy workforce-management products designed for broader healthcare organizations.

We believe our advantage is the combination of home-health-specific qualification logic, workflow integration, and the operational data created by repeated staffing decisions. However, incumbent scheduling vendors could add similar capabilities, and our current dataset is not yet large enough to establish a durable data advantage.
