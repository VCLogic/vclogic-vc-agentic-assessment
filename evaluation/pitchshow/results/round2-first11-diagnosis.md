# Round 2: Diagnosis of the First 11 Completed Assessments

Read-only diagnosis of frozen outputs: 3/11 decisions match the reference. All 11 Phase 2 outputs are provisional. This is a diagnostic snapshot, not a report that the entire batch has finished. No pipeline changes or paid diagnostic reruns were performed.

## Confirmed Cleanup Implementation Defect

`scripts/prepare_pitchshow_history.py:clean_record` requires `annotations[].pitch_evidence_verified is True`. One existing review format lacks that key and stores the independent verifier result under `verification.decisions[].pitch_decision_supported`.

85 installed-history exclusions were caused by this missing key. For all 85, the independent verifier explicitly supports the pitch decision, its evidence matches the annotation evidence, and a read-only reproduction using that verifier result passes all other existing cleaning checks (exact quotes, speakers, boundaries, and turn remapping). These are recoverable without restoring narration or weakening leakage exclusion. Current canonical corpus remains unchanged during the experiment.

Recoverable records: Beck 9, Charles 17, Cyan 4, Elizabeth 14, Howie 3, Jesse 3, Jillian 15, Mac 13, Mark 3, Paige 3, Phil 1. Beck currently has one accessible Out precedent in both completed runs. The ChurchSpace justification explicitly cites the lack of any analogous observed In as a reason not to activate the founder exception. The defect is confirmed; its causal effect on predictions has not been measured by a controlled rerun.

The previous 187 passing tests did not cover both historical review schemas. The earlier characterization of every excluded record as unverifiable was too strong.

## Evidence Applicability and Policy Selection

- **Beck / ChurchSpace:** the policy bundle has only one excerpt, describing the clients of BAM Communications, a PR business. The generated decision acknowledges it does not establish a current fund rule, yet uses the Series A/B profile as part of its stage uncertainty. ChurchSpace sanitization excludes 54 wiki sections; this demonstrates severely reduced policy coverage, not proof that every excluded section should be restored.
- **Jake / Hykso:** the decision activates a hard sector blocker from current Marque defense/national-security policy. The target is an older episode, and Jake's wiki explicitly distinguishes several firm eras. The source itself contains an era warning, but the decision applies the current mandate to the old observation. Later sources remain permitted: the needed correction is applicability to the investor's role/firm in the target episode, not a blanket ban on future publications.

## Why Everything Is Provisional

All 11 final decisions set `information_sufficient=false`, despite final decision streaks of 3–4. Seven record `PHASE2_NO_NOVELTY_STOP`; four reach the iteration limit without that marker. The workflow freezes a usable candidate as provisional when another search yields no meaningful novelty or the iteration cap is reached. This does not mean no assessment ran or that every final JSON is invalid.

There are also output-quality failures: among 164 saved Phase 1/2 model-response artifacts, 15 have `parsed=null`. Thirteen used the full 8,192 output tokens and two used 16,384; six contain large runs of invisible formatting characters. Their recorded cost is $0.204. Eight cases record at least one invalid Phase 1 candidate; three use a fallback retrieval plan. These artifacts demonstrate generation/structured-output problems; they do not establish whether the underlying cause lies in the model, upstream provider, or schema interaction.

## Recommended Order

1. Pause new launches after the current case, preserving every result. Do not silently replace this experiment's inputs mid-run.
2. Correct the review-format adapter; recover the 85 verified historical records through the same strict cleaner; test both schemas and report per-VC In/Out coverage.
3. Scope policy evidence by role, firm, era, and whether it is investment policy versus another business's description. Preserve source lineage for held-out exclusion.
4. Require substantive evidence that an exception precedent addresses the same controlling concern. Explicitly evaluate bottom-up venture economics; do not turn interest or generic founder quality into a commitment.
5. Diagnose token-cap/malformed responses and distinguish exhausted corpus searches from unanswered diligence. More iterations alone will not fix missing/wrong memory.
6. Run a small, separately labeled regression set before another broad batch; retain the original results and audit labels.

The running batch was left unchanged during this read-only diagnosis. Comparing this selection's 3/11 against the previous selection's 24/36 cannot isolate the effect of cleanup: the cases differ and the previous batch has confirmed leakage.
