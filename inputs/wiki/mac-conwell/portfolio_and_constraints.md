- RareBreed’s later primary description is pre-seed investing, primarily outside major technology ecosystems, with checks up to $250,000 as an initial or near-initial investor; historical interviews also included seed and selective later-stage exceptions. [ctx:mac-conwell-1773] [ev:mac-conwell-0295]

- The firm is generalist but consistently excludes life sciences/biotechnology because Conwell says the team lacks the technical assessment capability. [ev:mac-conwell-1202]

- Reported target checks ranged from $100,000 to $250,000, generally centered on $250,000; valuation preferences were often below $10 million post-money with selective flexibility, but neither is established as a current 2026 term. [ev:mac-conwell-0194] [ev:mac-conwell-0195]

- The firm historically required pro-rata rights, sought information rights, and did not generally require a board seat; later portfolio policy capped company exposure at 5% of the fund and $500,000. [ev:mac-conwell-0298] [ev:mac-conwell-1433]

- RareBreed’s financial mandate is venture equity in private companies capable of outsized exits; Conwell distinguishes this from lending, public-market investing, and alternative finance for non-venture businesses. [ev:mac-conwell-0765] [ev:mac-conwell-1811] [ev:mac-conwell-0839]

- Fund I was reported in 2025 testimony as having 194 investors and 47 company investments; earlier portfolio counts vary by source date and deployment stage rather than necessarily conflicting. [ctx:mac-conwell-1726] [ctx:mac-conwell-1727]

- Fund construction was constrained by LP-count rules, minimum-check economics, management-company operating capital, lack of a conventional personal GP commitment, and limited capacity for follow-on support. [ev:mac-conwell-0476] [ev:mac-conwell-0287] [ev:mac-conwell-2057]
