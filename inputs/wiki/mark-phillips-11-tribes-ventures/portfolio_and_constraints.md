## Mandate and constraints

- **Vehicle and stage:** 11 Tribes is an early-stage private venture investor, principally seed with occasional Series A exposure. Fund II was described as post-revenue under then-current conditions. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0209] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0124]

- **Check size evolved by fund:** Fund I sources report approximately $250,000–$300,000 initial checks; Fund II sources report $1 million–$1.5 million checks. A Signal aggregator’s $100,000–$300,000 range appears stale or vehicle-specific relative to later first-party Fund II data. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0065] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0127] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0283]

- **Revenue and valuation:** the recurring Fund II profile is approximately $500,000–$2 million of revenue, $2 million–$3 million rounds, and $10 million–$15 million pre-money pricing, with later descriptions extending post-money valuations to roughly $15 million–$25 million. These are strike-zone observations, not guaranteed hard gates. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0128] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0129] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0160]

- **Geography:** emphasis is North American, non-coastal, tier-two and tier-three markets, although early Fund I reporting included one Mexico investment. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0122] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0025]

- **Sector:** generalist and sector-diversified, with historical concentration in future of work, healthcare, data analytics, cybersecurity, food/agtech, and later vertical AI and blue-collar niches. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0074] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0140] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0192]

- **Exclusions:** vice industries such as alcohol and marijuana, plus AI wrappers and foundational LLM companies under the stated current posture. Religious identity is not an eligibility requirement. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0391] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0406] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0381]

- **Ownership and governance:** the fund generally seeks minority stakes, approximately 5–7% plus pro rata in one formulation and 5–10% in another. Later evidence says it increasingly leads and takes board influence. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0104] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0180] [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0138]

- **Return model:** approximately 10x valuation growth is sought, with acceptable exit bands varying from roughly $50 million in a sufficient cash-return case through $100–$300 million targets. The model does not require every company to become a unicorn. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0219] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0249] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0250]

- **Portfolio constraints:** existing sector concentration may block a new investment even without a direct conflict, and founder/lead-investor disagreement over growth and financing philosophy can end a deal. [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0102] [ev:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0169]

- **Founder-support commitment:** the fund allocates 2% of invested capital non-dilutively to founder resilience and organizational health. This is a firm program, not evidence of a personal philanthropic grant by Phillips. [ctx:mark-phillips-11-tribes-ventures-0096]
