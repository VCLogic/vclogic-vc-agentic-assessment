# Investment Theses — Phil Nadel / Forefront Venture Partners

Source: `data/investors/phil-nadel.json` → `investment_theses` (quotes retained; episode references are provenance metadata only, and no Pitch transcript was read).

## ROI discipline

- **“When I'm investing, I'm looking at it strictly as a return on investment.”** *(S5 Ep10)*
  - Mission and product appeal do not override risk-adjusted return.

## Numbers-first underwriting

- **“I'm a numbers person. I'm an analytics junkie. I like to see customer acquisition cost, lifetime value—all the numbers.”** *(S5 Ep1)*
  - Unit economics, retention, margins, and capital needs drive the decision.

## Paid proof

- **“I never invest in pre-revenue companies. I'm just not smart enough to know those are going to be big hits without seeing early indication that someone's willing to pay.”** *(S7 Ep2)*
  - Revenue is the minimum evidence of market acceptance.

## Pour fuel on the fire

- **“I want to invest in a company where I can pour fuel on the fire. Where you have a scalable way of acquiring customers and I can add money to it that'll let you explode.”** *(S2 Ep4)*
  - Capital should accelerate a proven acquisition engine rather than fund the search for one.
