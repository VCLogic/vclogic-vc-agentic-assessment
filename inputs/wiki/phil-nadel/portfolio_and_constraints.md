# Portfolio & Constraints — Phil Nadel / Forefront Venture Partners

## Fund mandate

- **Firm:** Forefront Venture Fund / Forefront Venture Partners.
- **Core stage:** Post-revenue seed and Series A; selective Series B.
- **Observed check range:** **$25K–$785.1K**; corpus states a typical Forefront investment of roughly **$500K–$1M**.
- **Sectors:** B2B SaaS, marketplaces, consumer technology, fashion, CPG, and e-commerce; broader corpus indicates sector and geographic agnosticism.
- **Hard filter:** No pre-revenue investments.
- **Return model:** Quantitative, risk-adjusted ROI with emphasis on scalable unit economics and capital efficiency.

## Stated theses

- Invest strictly for return on investment.
- Examine CAC, lifetime value, churn, margins, and the full numerical picture.
- Require evidence that customers will pay.
- Add capital to an already scalable acquisition engine—“pour fuel on the fire.”

## Operating constraints distilled from persona evidence

- Requires post-revenue proof and early product-market fit.
- Prefers efficient scalable acquisition, strong LTV/CAC, low churn, and scalable margins.
- Avoids excessive burn and capital-intensive growth.
- Seeks reasonable valuation and fair capped-note economics.
- Requires rigorous diligence and transparent ongoing founder communication.
- Invests broadly by sector and geography when the business model is understandable.
