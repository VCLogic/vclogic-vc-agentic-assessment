### Mandate and constraints

- Accomplice’s source-era mandate was early-stage technology, with stated first checks of $1 million–$2.5 million for 10%–20% ownership; these parameters are historical and should not be replaced by aggregator ranges of up to $10 million. [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0242] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0080]
- As of January 2020, Downey said she was not writing Accomplice checks, despite continuing portfolio, board, Rev Boston, and Spearhead work; later or undated “current” profiles cannot safely override that dated first-person statement without a newer dated receipt. [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0172]
- Yubari’s separate mandate was $10,000–$100,000 pre-seed checks, mostly in gaming, crypto, and digital health; its reported first-fund performance was unrealized, not realized cash returns. [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0298] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0176]
- One transcript dates her first angel check to July 14, 2017, while a written account summarizes five checks since February 2017; the timing has not been independently reconciled. Her source counts evolve from five, to 20, 30, approximately 40, and media claims of 50-plus, reflecting different dates and possibly mixed personal/fund-backed definitions rather than a stable portfolio count. [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0014] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0129] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0175] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0008] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0118]
- Personal angel investing is portfolio-based: she advised capacity for roughly 20 investments over several years because most fail and a small number of outliers drive returns. [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0181] [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0182]
- Portfolio conflicts can prevent investment, and high capital intensity may exceed a particular fund’s capacity rather than proving the company itself uninvestable. [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0244] [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0331]
- Smaller angels often follow a lead’s terms, and although pro-rata rights are valuable, exercising them can become too expensive as successful companies appreciate. [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0169] [ev:sarah-downey-accomplice-0171]
- Founder, advisor, board, interim executive, nonprofit, media, sponsorship, employment, and program roles above are not treated as holdings unless a source separately reports an investment relationship. [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0187] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0040] [ctx:sarah-downey-accomplice-0110]

No supplied evidence supports majority ownership or acquisition by Downey, a real-estate portfolio, a public-markets portfolio, or LP commitments to outside funds.
