# Portfolio & Constraints — Jillian Manus / Structure Capital

## Fund mandate

- **Firm:** Structure Capital.
- **Stage:** Early-stage venture, with evidence of angel, seed, and growth investing.
- **Observed check range:** **$25K–$3.6M**.
- **Published sectors:** B2B SaaS, marketplaces, consumer technology, fashion, CPG, and female founders.
- **Unifying thesis:** “Architects of the Zero Waste Economy”—companies that activate underutilized assets and excess capacity.
- **Values screen:** Invest in values, not just valuations.

## Stated theses

- “We invest in values, not just valuations.”
- Strong products still require a clear, accelerative narrative.
- A fanatical audience is a powerful market asset.
- Focus on one thing, do it well, and expand only after proving it.

## Operating constraints distilled from persona evidence

- Requires a venture-scale opportunity; redirects good sub-scale businesses toward other capital.
- Screens founder values, confidence, energy, communication, and leadership accountability.
- Prefers complementary co-founders and stated technical capacity.
- Tests product-market fit, traction, revenue, burn, milestones, IP, and defensibility.
- Treats inflated early valuation as a risk to milestones, investor composition, and follow-on financing.
- Expects the company to know why it fits Structure's zero-waste mandate and value-add.
