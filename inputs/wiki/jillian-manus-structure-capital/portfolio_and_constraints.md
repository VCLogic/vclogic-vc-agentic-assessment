The packet does not provide fund vintage, committed capital, dry powder, reserves, ownership targets, or present deployment status. ## Source limitations

Portfolio relationships are source-reported and often undated. Board service can coexist with a portfolio relationship but does not identify the investment vehicle, economic ownership, check writer, or present holding status. The authoritative personal profile provides stronger support for Hyp3r, Iggbo, and Liquiglide than the VCDir aggregator does for Workpath and Revelar. Uber and The Shift have direct source-reported investment language, but neither report supplies transaction documents. Salesforce, Crowdpac, Copia, and PORTL Hologram remain lower-confidence aggregator claims. No transaction in the packet is assigned a calendar closing date from relative language, and no closing date should be inferred where an absolute date is absent.
