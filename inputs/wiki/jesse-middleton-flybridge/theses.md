# Investment Theses — Jesse Middleton / Flybridge

Source: `data/investors/jesse-middleton-flybridge.json` → `investment_theses` (quotes retained; episode references are provenance metadata only, and no Pitch transcript was read).

## Build for a reason

- **“I am so sick of people that are building stuff for building stuff's sake.”** *(S14 Ep2)*
  - Technology must solve a meaningful customer or societal problem.

## Returns and positive impact

- **“There are some companies where we can make a lot of money on it, but it's also great for the world. And if it only returns half my fund, I'd still feel really great about backing that company.”** *(S13 Ep1)*
  - Mission can matter alongside fund economics.

## Bet on exceptional founders

- **“If they're incredible, sometimes you just gotta bet on them. They're gonna figure it out.”** *(S14; episode reference unspecified in source)*
  - Founder quality can justify pre-seed uncertainty.

## Repeatable early traction

- **“You need to show that you have figured out some really strong, early, repeatable traction model.”** *(S13 Ep9)*
  - Early progress must contain a repeatable acquisition or retention mechanism.
