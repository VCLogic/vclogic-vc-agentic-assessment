# Portfolio & Constraints — Jesse Middleton / Flybridge

## Fund mandate

- **Firm:** Flybridge.
- **Stage:** Pre-seed and seed; corpus describes leading seed rounds with roughly $1M–$3M checks, while The Pitch deployment data records $20K–$50K checks.
- **Themes:** Future of work, creativity, culture, collaboration, communication, community-driven companies, and current native/applied/infrastructure AI.
- **Network Funds:** Distributed investment partners extend access to female, underestimated, university, operator, and regional founder communities.
- **Return model:** Venture-scale LP returns, with willingness to value companies that also create meaningful social or community benefit.

## Stated theses

- Do not build technology for its own sake.
- A company can be financially strong and good for the world.
- Exceptional founders sometimes deserve a bet before everything is figured out.
- Seek strong, early, repeatable traction.

## Operating constraints distilled from persona evidence

- Founder and market carry more weight than the current product at pre-seed.
- Requires focus, feasibility evidence, customer learning, and a venture-scale outcome.
- Treats authentic community as a potential growth, retention, and product moat.
- Prefers founder-controlled seed governance and reasonable capitalization.
- Avoids legitimate portfolio conflicts.
- Expects Flybridge's network and community to provide value beyond capital.
