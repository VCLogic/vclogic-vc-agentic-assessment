## Fund allocation and portfolio construction

- **Explicit/source-reported — power-law diversification:** The 2018 synopsis reports Nadel’s heuristic that, among 100 startup investments, about 70 fail, 20–30 stagnate or return roughly 1–3x, and perhaps one or two become home runs; this underpins his warning against tiny angel portfolios. [ev:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0001] [ctx:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0013]
- **Source-reported — rolling-fund structure:** In the July 16, 2024 interview, Nadel described progressing from small-scale family-and-friends investing to a syndicate and then an AngelList rolling fund; he described himself as GP, deciding with an investment committee, and said the fund charged carry without management or upfront fees. - **Explicit — active ownership:** Investor success combines returns with helping entrepreneurs grow; Forefront reportedly supplies customer, talent, reseller, technology-partner, and fundraising connections and expects structured company reporting. [ev:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0049] [ctx:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0049] [ctx:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0050] [ctx:phil-nadel-forefront-venture-partners-0073]
