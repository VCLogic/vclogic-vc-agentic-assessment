## Mandate and operating constraints

- October 2023 material reported that Marque was raising $100 million for Fund I. This is a historical fundraising target, not confirmation of a final close, current AUM or available capital. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0273]


- **Current mandate:** Marque reports Seed-to-Series A investing in emerging capabilities with a clear defense application for the United States and allies, while Chapman describes the firm as almost exclusively national-security technology and usually government-facing. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0278] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0279] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0245] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0246] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0247]

- **Stage history:** Gelt was seed-stage; Alpha Bridge focused post-seed/pre-Series A and used very small earlier checks mainly to build relationships; Marque Fund One ranged from pre-seed through Series A, with a later plan to emphasize Series A. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0169] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0207] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0210] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0037] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0040]

- **Geography:** Alpha Bridge stated a North American mandate and reported investments in Canada and several U.S. cities. Marque’s market orientation is the United States, NATO, and allies, which is a customer/use constraint rather than necessarily a precise incorporation rule. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0217] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0045] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0092] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0093] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0270]

- **Check size:** A Signal profile reports a $100,000–$5 million range and $1.5 million sweet spot, while Evalyze repeats the $1.5 million figure. These are aggregator-reported parameters tied to Chapman/Alpha Bridge-era profiles, not verified current Marque terms. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0307] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0283]

- **Growth-stage boundary:** Chapman says Marque generally does not invest at growth stage, though it may facilitate growth opportunities for interested LPs. [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0054]

- **Capital reserve constraint:** Marque’s inability to fund every later round makes Series B financeability a present diligence consideration; Alpha Bridge similarly faced the risk of losing follow-on access to larger investors. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0024] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0025] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0155] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0209]

- **Portfolio conflicts and selectivity:** An existing investment in a competitor can block a deal, and Chapman has described choosing roughly four investments from 1,000–1,500 reviewed opportunities in one year. Those funnel figures are source-specific, not a standing annual quota. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0183] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0185]

- **Sourcing:** He reports no investment arising from a purely cold email, but has invested after relationships began on Twitter or through a relevant portfolio-founder referral. Tailored outreach and long-term relationship formation are more consistent with his stated behavior than generic inbound. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0264] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0267] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0268] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0269] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0121]

- **Pitch mechanics:** A deck is required in advance, though the live meeting may be conversational. The deck should quickly establish the unique value proposition, customer, business model, differentiation, and credible large-market path. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0270] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0413] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0414] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0416]

- **Non-dilutive funding:** Chapman welcomes grants when they fund the existing roadmap and do not divert scarce people, but warns that grants and R&D money are not themselves procurement traction. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0244] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0031] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0334] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0335]

- **Ethical and geopolitical limits:** Marque rejects intended customers or uses inconsistent with its ethical standards, assesses weapons through intent and use, restricts emerging capabilities to U.S. and allied users, and Chapman strongly opposes investment in China. [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0132] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0277] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0293] [ev:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0294] [ctx:jake-chapman-marque-ventures-0247]
