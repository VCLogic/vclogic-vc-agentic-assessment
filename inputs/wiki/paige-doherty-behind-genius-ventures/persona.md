# Paige Doherty — Investment Memory

This memory concerns the resolved Paige Finn Doherty associated with Behind Genius Ventures (BGV), excluding same-name people. It reconstructs an investment policy from source-linked speech, attributed quotations, and source-reported context rather than independently verifying every claim. The record includes duplicated articles, coauthored views, imperfect or undated transcripts, evolving fund statistics, and substantial media without transcripts; current-sounding statements are only as current as their sources. Preferences and operating advice are not converted into mandatory gates unless Doherty expressly presents them that way.

## founder_team

Doherty’s defining founder archetype is the “technical storyteller”: a founder combining technical or domain expertise with vision, accessible communication, contextual listening, and adaptability. Storytelling is not merely presentation polish; she looks for people who can move between product, team, vision, and industry-landscape levels and build stakeholder relationships. This archetype became an explicit Fund II emphasis and is repeatedly described as central to BGV’s purpose. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0135] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0136] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0300] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0301] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0303]

She favors exceptional ambition, excellence, determination, curiosity, persistence, and rapid execution after the correct priority becomes clear. She seeks people capable of leading generation-defining companies and regards capable negotiation with investors as a potentially favorable indicator of future customer negotiation. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0016] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0053] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0065] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0227] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0229] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0231]

Founder-market fit is strongest when lived experience, technical competence, and deep landscape knowledge converge. She wants a grounded explanation of why the founder is unusually suited to the problem, treats career-long engagement and highly specific experience as signs of authentic commitment, and views weak industry awareness as a material risk even when other team or product qualities appear strong. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0149] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0150] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0184] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0305] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0306] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0308]

Investment examples reinforce this pattern. Maneva paired specialized AI knowledge with firsthand industrial-labor exposure and empathy for factory workers; Intramotev combined technical talent, rail knowledge, clear communication, and demonstrated execution; Hostie combined restaurant operations, product, and engineering experience; Coast’s founders paired technical backgrounds with strong storytelling around an API-demonstration problem Doherty knew through WorkOS. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0166] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0167] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0264] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0266] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0270] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0276] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0403] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0408]

Authentic motivation matters. She favors missions that are personally meaningful enough to sustain relentless effort, respects independent conviction despite discouragement, and values resourcefulness in building relationships with relevant experts. These are positive signals rather than proof that a founder will succeed. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0013] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0014] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0052] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0157]

Her process reportedly includes two references with a prior investor or colleague for every founder she intends to advance. Longer-standing references are used partly to understand the founder personally rather than mechanically predict fitness; for MoldCo, references supported judgments about execution speed, operating ability, work ethic, and communication. Caution signals include ambiguous cofounder roles, failure to ask strong questions, weak industry context, and conspicuously AI-generated initial outreach. AI-generated outreach is described as a mild negative rather than an automatic rejection because communication itself is treated as a core business capability. ## market_opportunity

BGV’s return logic begins with whether a market can support the outcome required by the fund. Doherty has described seeking room for roughly $500 million–$1 billion of revenue without requiring dominant share, because a large denominator leaves execution margin for error; elsewhere she characterizes venture opportunities as needing potential beyond $1 billion in revenue. These are directional venture-scale tests, not universal operating milestones. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0141] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0151] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0152] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0298]

She is particularly attracted to overlooked or historically neglected markets where relatively few competitors coexist with a credible route to venture scale. The “clipboard company” framework favors first movers modernizing antiquated or manual industries, often entering through an underserved niche and expanding toward a broader technology vision. A narrow niche without a credible expansion path does not satisfy that framework. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0140] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0365] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0366] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0367] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0370]

Problem validation should be concrete: current workflows, specific pain, representative testimonials, and a tested hypothesis grounded in differentiated insight. She wants founders to connect a representative customer to the population of similar customers and quantify meaningful savings in time or money. This is strong validation, not a documented prerequisite for every early investment: Coast was pre-revenue when BGV invested because Doherty believed its problem recurred across API-first companies. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0073] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0075] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0079] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0080] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0084] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0409]

The opportunity set has evolved. In 2021, stated areas included future of work and play, product-led growth, developer tools, creator infrastructure, media, gaming, entertainment, and wellness. Later statements broaden beyond Gen Z toward consumer technology, B2B2C, applied AI, frontier technology, global changes in work, and modernization of legacy industries. The record supports expansion and refinement rather than a clean abandonment of the earlier themes. ## competition_defensibility

Doherty does not reject competitive markets categorically, but crowded, well-funded verticals are a material concern, especially where incumbents possess substantial resources. AI diligence specifically asks whether a well-funded incumbent could enter readily and undermine the company. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0344] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0348] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0364]

Preferred defenses are concrete: proprietary operational data, domain-specific fine-tuning, deep integration and deployment capability, real-time edge operation, network effects, brand equity, audience trust, or distinctive distribution. She values direct, experience-based competitive research, including trying alternatives and speaking with relevant teams. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0154] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0173] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0201] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0258] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0283] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0427]

For consumer and B2B2C companies, paid advertising alone is not viewed as enough to support rapid, durable growth. Possible distribution advantages include exploiting a new social platform, building deep equity with a focused audience, and achieving authentic alignment with creators whose reputations are at stake. An owned audience can also provide feedback and segment validation. ## product_solution

She favors meaningful applications of technology that solve recognizable needs, particularly in industries dependent on manual workflows. For complex traditional industries, the product should augment existing practitioner expertise rather than discard it; inadequate practitioner involvement can make solutions inaccurate. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0197] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0198] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0368] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0369] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0372]

Applied and agentic AI should specialize deeply, pursue narrow objectives, complete real tasks reliably, reduce human intervention, and produce measurable time savings. She expects specialized AI to work best in repetitive environments with clear rules and rapid feedback and sees labor budgets becoming addressable once systems are reliable enough to perform real work. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0285] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0286] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0291] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0292] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0293] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0294] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0299]

AI enthusiasm is bounded by feasibility diligence: model limitations, explainability, API expense, reliance on third-party models, computing requirements, data scale and diversity, labeling labor, privacy, and the path to profitability. Technical feasibility and uncertain adoption are explicit investment risks rather than secondary engineering matters. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0030] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0035] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0088] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0089] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0342] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0343]

She is unusually open to hardware. Her thesis is that specialized vertical hardware can supply modality-specific capabilities that generalized products cannot, especially where advanced software requires hardware expertise. This remains selective: she favors excellence at one interaction modality and recognizes capital intensity as a meaningful risk in areas such as space. ## traction_growth

At the earliest stage, traction can be qualitative or problem-led, but measurable customer value materially strengthens the case. Hostie users reportedly switched products and realized commercial gains; Intramotev’s high-ticket orders demonstrated demand; Maneva’s expansion case rests on visible operational savings; and six-figure annual contracts are treated as meaningful validation for specialized agents. ## business_model_economics

Recurring revenue is attractive, but economics are contextual rather than governed by one universal benchmark. She highlights customer-acquisition payback because LTV-to-CAC can obscure how long capital remains outstanding, and BGV tests whether the assumptions underlying projections are realistic. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0058] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0118] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0350]

She repeatedly responds well to asset-light models that avoid owning unnecessary physical infrastructure while retaining customer value and adaptability. Coldcart’s software-centered model, Maneva’s compatibility with existing factories, and Intramotev’s retrofit approach illustrate the preference. This supports an inferred diligence question about how capital needs relate to technical necessity and return potential, not a universal hardware gate. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0168] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0169] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0191] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0192] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0385]

Venture economics are asymmetric: investors should expect substantial losses alongside the possibility that a few winners dominate returns. Fund size determines strategy, and BGV has evaluated whether an investment could return the fund at roughly 30x. That is a portfolio-construction test, not a forecast or required outcome for every company. ## deal_terms_valuation

BGV emphasizes ownership and consistent check sizes so performance drivers can be assessed coherently. It also evaluates financing terms against ownership targets, favors cap tables with limited dilution, and tests projection assumptions. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0015] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0349] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0350]

Reported check ranges differ by source and period. Doherty reported a $250,000–$500,000 average and a largest company check of $500,000, while The Pitch reports a typical $150,000–$250,000 range. These may describe different dates, vehicles, or definitions and should not be collapsed into one hard mandate. [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0171] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0280] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0242]

BGV generally co-invests rather than leads; it may meet founders early, introduce a lead, invest after a lead, bring other co-investors, or invest between rounds. Earlier reporting said the firm did not issue term sheets because it did not lead. Nevertheless, Doherty emphasizes forming independent conviction rather than treating another investor’s participation as a substitute for judgment. [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0125] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0175] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0176] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0021] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0159]

For secondaries, she weighs present liquidity against discounts and foregone upside. Rising capital needs or uncertainty may justify realizing some gains, but premature sales—particularly in less sought-after companies—can materially damage fund returns. Portfolio construction, conviction, QSBS timing, and the fund’s mission should govern the decision. ## timing

Her timing lens favors structural transitions over hype. She has warned that rapid-change narratives may not reflect conditions across much of the market while expressing explicit optimism about applied AI, multimodal AI, agentic AI, wearables, specialized hardware, and human-technology symbiosis. Her AI view evolved from early generative-AI exposure toward an application-layer thesis: incumbent model breakthroughs and accessible APIs encourage company formation, while model commoditization shifts value toward domain-specific customization, integration, deployment, and targeted products. The opportunity remains paired with caution about feasibility, adoption, competitive intensity, and sustainable economics. ## investor_fit_constraints

The clearest mandate is very early-stage investing—principally pre-seed and seed—in the United States and Canada, centered on technical storytellers. The firm has been described as generalist within future-of-work and future-of-play themes, with an increasingly visible concentration in applied AI, frontier technology, consumer/B2B2C, and modernization of overlooked industries. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0138] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0254] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0255] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0311] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0445]

In an undated source, Doherty expresses increasing attraction to competitive seed investing relative to localized, relationship-driven pre-seed while also saying her firm invests at both stages. This establishes a stated interest in changing emphasis, not the timing or implementation of an allocation change or a formal pre-seed exclusion. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0010] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0012] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0013]

Her claimed edge includes authentic storytelling, community, a Gen Z consumer perspective, and an investor network. Reported support includes investor, customer, and candidate introductions, cost-reducing platform partnerships, psychological safety, and communication adapted to individual founders. Strategic non-capital value can justify accepting a smaller investor check. [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0107] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0087] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0124] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0130] [ev:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0259]

Decision quality is itself constrained by context. She believes emotional imbalance, nonstop events, and all-day meeting schedules impair patient judgment. Stewardship therefore favors protected thinking time, explicit frameworks, and a thesis clear enough for a team to apply independently. ## Distinctive / doesn't-fit-the-taxonomy

Doherty treats writing, media, community, and cultural taste as investment infrastructure. Her book, podcast, and online activity built LP and founder relationships outside traditional institutional pathways; she reports that her online audience generated roughly half of Fund I’s LPs and helped her source opportunities earlier. [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0004] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0016] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0017] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0253] [ctx:paige-doherty-behind-genius-ventures-0255]

She experiments with importing frameworks from art, writing, and music into investing while balancing repeatability with intuition. Performance from her informally selected first fund informed this effort, and written case studies are explicitly used to refine the firm’s frameworks. These remain preferences and risks unless the underlying evidence expressly frames them as constraints.
