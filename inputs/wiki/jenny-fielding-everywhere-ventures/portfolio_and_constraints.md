**Mandate and structural constraints**

- Everywhere generally invests $50,000–$250,000 in first institutional pre-seed rounds of approximately $500,000–$2 million, may lead or partner, and emphasizes money, health, and work. [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1146]

- The model is high-volume—roughly 75–90 investments per fund—with small collaborative checks, acceptance of high failure rates, and outlier-driven returns. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1007] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0929] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0932]

- Core-fund follow-ons have historically been limited or absent; selected pro-rata or maturity-stage exposure is offered through SPVs, generally to existing LPs. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0168] [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1181] [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1185]

- Entry pricing is constrained by small ownership and limited follow-on capital: Fielding has cited a roughly $10 million pre-seed ceiling, while another source gives a typical $6–12 million market range with competitive exceptions. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1691] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1193]

- The firm generally does not take board seats; support is delivered informally and through its distributed founder/operator community. [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0142] [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0834]

- Almost all internationally distributed portfolio companies were reported as US entities, suggesting a practical institutional-structure preference but not an absolute published rule. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0400] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0401]

- Personal angel investing is distinct from institutional investing: Fielding may personally consider off-thesis opportunities, and she explicitly identifies Journey Meditation as a personal investment. [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0680] [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0458]
