# Jenny Fielding — Recurring Investment Theses

- **Global pre-seed / direct investment — explicit:** Everywhere seeks to be the first institutional check in companies raising roughly $500,000–$2 million and does not confine sourcing to established technology hubs; locally embedded founder networks supply context, references, and support. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0474] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0686] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1218]

- **Founder-first underwriting — explicit:** Before product-market fit, Fielding prioritizes exceptional people, distinctive future vision, resilience, ethical character, learning speed, and decisive action over confidence in the initial product or a narrow sector forecast. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1419] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0914] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0460]

- **Empath-founder pattern — explicit with caution:** Personal or professional experience of the problem is a strong source of insight and commitment, but attachment becomes negative when founders ignore contrary evidence; openness and a learning mindset are the counterweight. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1588] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1590] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1591]

- **People over rigid theses — explicit:** At the earliest stage, she may prioritize an exceptional founder over present market-size skepticism or rigid thematic fit because products and categories frequently change. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1252] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1253] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1256]

- **Table-stakes / resilience economy — explicit, evolving:** Recurring institutional themes are money, health, and work, broadened elsewhere to climate, logistics, automation, SaaS, and enabling software or infrastructure that makes institutions more resilient. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1235] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1352] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1561]

- **Application-layer AI — explicit:** AI is not a standalone Everywhere thesis; Fielding prefers application-layer and vertical-software companies solving real problems, particularly in antiquated industries, and generally avoids foundational AI infrastructure. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0148] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0736] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1592] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1593]

- **AI efficiency and durability — explicit:** She expects teams to use modern AI tools efficiently and asks how a company remains relevant as models improve; distribution, integrations, regulatory progress, and proprietary source material are more persuasive moat candidates than generic AI or data alone. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0027] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1367] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1083]

- **Healthcare infrastructure — explicit:** Healthcare is a large, consequential market, with particular interest in enabling infrastructure, payments, automation, and modernization rather than direct delivery; incentives, regulation, integrations, and payer behavior remain central risks. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0552] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1397] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0887]

- **Fintech through cycles — explicit:** Fielding has invested in fintech since 2015 and remains long-term bullish; cooled sentiment and lower prices can create attractive entry conditions, while incumbent trust, lending economics, and regulatory execution remain constraints. [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0451] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0740] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0214] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1243]

- **Future of work and legacy-industry automation — explicit:** Longstanding interests include technology for independent workers, changing work, automation, and vertical SaaS modernizing inefficient industries such as logistics and manufacturing. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0227] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0553] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0032]

- **Climate — explicit but secondary:** Climate appears within the broader resilience thesis; she cites European regulation and possible substitution of private capital for declining government support as favorable tailwinds. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0150] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1647] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1648]

- **Developing and emerging markets — explicit:** Fielding expects substantial innovation and opportunity outside mature hubs, with specific enthusiasm for Brazil, Mexico, Nigeria, Africa, and Latin America, while requiring credible local relationships and support. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1314] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1541] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1218]

- **Capital-efficient company building — explicit:** She favors lean staffing, efficient acquisition, sound unit economics, financial command, and enough runway to reach milestones without assuming another round. Excess capital can weaken discipline, while an inadequately small raise can also create risk. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0187] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0021] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1196] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1327]

- **Venture fit versus good-business fit — explicit:** Venture suits highly scalable, high-growth businesses with very large return potential; attractive small, service, or slower profitability-oriented businesses may belong elsewhere in the capital stack. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1278] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1279] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1303]

- **High-volume fund construction — explicit:** Everywhere uses approximately 75–90 investments per fund, accepts many failures, and seeks rare outliers capable of returning the fund. Small size and low entry prices also allow smaller exits to matter. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1007] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0818] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1688] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0970]

- **Community-powered venture — explicit:** Founder and operator LPs contribute sourcing, specialist diligence, references, introductions, and scalable portfolio support rather than serving solely as capital providers. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0105] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0661] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1241]

- **Long-horizon contrarian timing — explicit:** Fielding assesses markets over roughly a decade, favors continuing to invest through downturns and unpopular sectors, and asks for both present timing and long-term relevance. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0962] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1104] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1362]

- **Direct angel investing — explicit and distinct:** Personal angel investments may fall outside Everywhere’s thesis and succeed at smaller exits than an institutional fund requires; she has also described personal cryptocurrency investments prompted by learning from founders. [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0680] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0836] [ctx:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0682]

- **Fund allocation / LP exposure — explicit:** For venture allocators, she favors a small, diversified, long-term allocation across vintages, informed acceptance of illiquidity and total-loss risk, and manager evaluation against vintage benchmarks rather than brands or fashionable themes. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1408] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1409] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1415] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1417]

- **Public markets — explicit and limited:** Fielding disclaims public-market authority because she focuses on early stage; in the cited AI discussion she would not recommend buying the referenced OpenAI or Anthropic shares and considered very high entry valuations risky. This is not a general public-equity strategy. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0528] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1501] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1503]

- **Real estate — no affirmative thesis established:** Her expressly described investment concentrations are venture-stage technology areas such as money, health, work, climate, automation, and SaaS; the supplied evidence reports no real-estate allocation or property-investment framework. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1689] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1561]

- **Operating-company strategy — inferred unless expressly stated:** Her advice favors founder-built financial models, focused distribution, incremental validation, scenario planning, a credible route to sustainability, and venture capital as an accelerator rather than a permanent substitute for customer demand. These principles inform diligence but are not universal investment prerequisites. [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-0489] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1114] [ev:jenny-fielding-everywhere-ventures-1460]
