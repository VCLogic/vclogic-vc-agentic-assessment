## Mandate and constraints

- The family office is not a venture fund and has no obligation to deploy, permitting long periods without direct deals and a high rejection rate. [ev:michael-hyatt-0022] [ev:michael-hyatt-0517]

- Allocation descriptions differ: one account gives approximately one-third each to public markets, real estate, and private equity including venture; another describes public equities, private equity with less venture, and acquisitions or major direct stakes. Markets Group separately describes public equities, private equity strategies and late-stage venture as the three buckets. These are source-specific descriptions, not a reconciled current mandate or established chronology. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0491] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0492] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0574] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0101] [ev:michael-hyatt-0413]

- Direct investments are concentrated, active, and generally require a credible way for Hyatt’s operating experience, board work, or customer access to improve the outcome. [ev:michael-hyatt-0020] [ev:michael-hyatt-0069] [ev:michael-hyatt-0162]

- The direct geography explicitly includes the United States; Europe was described as harder to cover. Aggregator claims of much broader global reach should not override this direct statement. [ev:michael-hyatt-0261] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0380]

- Direct sector attention stays largely within areas Hyatt understands; he specifically excludes clean technology and health technology from his own competence set despite having broad market curiosity and indirect diversified exposure. [ev:michael-hyatt-0415] [ev:michael-hyatt-0416]

- Northleaf’s mandate is distinct: larger, later-stage, profitable businesses within its venture-capital and growth-equity program, rather than Hyatt’s full personal or family-office opportunity set. [ev:michael-hyatt-0230] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0177]

- Public equities follow a passive, long-horizon, buy-and-hold posture, while private operating investments must offer returns sufficient to compensate for illiquidity, risk, and hands-on work. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0120] [ev:michael-hyatt-0412] [ev:michael-hyatt-0469]

- Founder or adviser status must not be treated as evidence of portfolio ownership, and BlueCat’s reported $11M and $16.8M company financings do not establish a personal investment by Hyatt. [ctx:michael-hyatt-0139] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0504] [ctx:michael-hyatt-0213]

The record remains incomplete: several interviews lack dates, transaction amounts and investment vehicles are often omitted, some named relationships may have ended, and database listings conflict. Relative timing phrases have not been converted into calendar closing dates; notably, the Nov. 1, 2023 DataStealth article’s phrase “earlier that month” leaves the absolute closing date unresolved.
