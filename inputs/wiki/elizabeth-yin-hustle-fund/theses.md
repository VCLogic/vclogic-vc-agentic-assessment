- **Explicit — Software mandate with recurring verticals:** Hustle Fund is described as squarely software, commonly including business software, digital health, and fintech; a later formulation calls the strategy global, generalist, pre-seed, valuation-sensitive, and largely focused on picks-and-shovels that can monetize early. ## Fund allocation and portfolio construction

- **Explicit — Power-law diversification:** The pre-product-market-fit strategy takes many capped-downside shots because a small number of outsized winners must offset numerous losses. - **Explicit — Volume requires selection and infrastructure:** A large portfolio only works with strong dealflow, disciplined comparative selection, and sufficient processes, technology, and staff; investing indiscriminately produces diminishing returns. - **Explicit — Small fund checks are investments, not options:** Yin contrasts her approximately $200,000 pre-seed example with billion-dollar multistage funds whose small seed checks can function as options, making Hustle Fund more sensitive to selection and valuation.
