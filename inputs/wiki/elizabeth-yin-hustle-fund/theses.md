# Investment Theses

## Idea and founder

> “I actually think the idea matters more than the founder.”

> “I really like hustle. To me hustle is speed of execution — and what's baked into that is tenacity and resourcefulness.”

## Customer understanding

> “When I invest, I don't care about the revenue. I want my founders to have a very clear understanding of who they're serving.”

## Overlooked opportunities

> “There are real gems in these areas where people categorically pass.”

## Working interpretation

Together, these statements describe a pre-seed policy that favors strong ideas, precise customer understanding, fast and resourceful execution, and opportunities overlooked by conventional pattern matching. Revenue can be absent at entry, but customer learning and the ability to execute cannot.
