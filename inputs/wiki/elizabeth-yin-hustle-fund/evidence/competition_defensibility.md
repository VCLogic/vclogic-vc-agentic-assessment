[ev:yin-0020] label=product_differentiation_concern direction=negative source=blog:on-differentiation
> "Differentiation is very difficult — there are so many entrepreneurs who are doing something similar to you and also so many alternatives that achieve the same results as your product / solution."

[ev:yin-0021] label=competitive_advantage_assessment direction=positive source=blog:on-differentiation
> "Once you’ve established why your company / product is different, think about how you’ll continue to be different. Investors call this your moat."

[ev:yin-0022] label=competitive_landscape_inertia direction=neutral source=blog:heres-why-understanding-switching-costs-is-crucial-to-good-product-strategy
> "The cost of switching to a product determines adoption . The cost of switching away from a product determines retention."

[ev:yin-0023] label=product_differentiation_concern direction=positive source=blog:build-a-product-that-fits-your-runway
> "You need to really think carefully about how to build a simple and quick-to-build product that will compete in an existing space through strong differentiation."

[ev:yin-0024] label=competitive_advantage_assessment direction=negative source=blog:deepseek_disruption_ai_nvidia_and_the_future_of_venture_capital
> "If anyone can spin up an AI-powered product in a weekend, it’s difficult to build a defensible business."

[ev:yin-0025] label=competitive_advantage_assessment direction=positive source=blog:deepseek_disruption_ai_nvidia_and_the_future_of_venture_capital
> "There are going to be software businesses that are so entrenched in workflow that even if they are copied, the sheer distribution edge is enough to win."

[ev:yin-0026] label=competitive_advantage_assessment direction=positive source=blog:the_artificial_intelligence_driven_future_who_wins_and_who_loses
> "Everyone will be forced to build a brand; when software becomes a commodity, those with large reach will win regardless of the field."

[ev:yin-0027] label=competitive_intensity_concern direction=negative source=talk:40Wrak60R7I
