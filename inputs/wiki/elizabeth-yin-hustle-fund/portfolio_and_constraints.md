## Mandate and allocation constraints

- **Hustle Fund mandate:** The direct record places the firm in global, very-early software investing, with recurring emphasis on business software, digital health, and fintech; a later description adds generalist, picks-and-shovels, early monetization, and valuation sensitivity. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0023] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0132]

- **Stage:** Companies may lack revenue and traction, but ordinarily should have progressed beyond a newly conceived idea through a bare product, prototype, landing page, customer conversations, conversions, LOIs, or presales. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0048] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0067] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0068] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0142]

- **Seed exposure:** A later source says the fund remained pre-seed-focused while making about 20% of investments at seed and using Angel Squad SPVs for follow-ons. [ctx:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0132]

- **Geography:** Yin says Hustle Fund invests globally; source snapshots specifically mention substantial activity in Canada and Southeast Asia, while she also observes that the United States tends to support larger exits. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0026] [ctx:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0096]

- **Return construction:** The fund seeks roughly 100x potential because many portfolio companies will fail and a small number of winners must dominate returns. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0009] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0043] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0135]

- **Valuation:** Yin prefers low entry valuations and at one source moment said Hustle Fund strongly preferred below $10 million post-money, while allowing higher prices where de-risking or outcome potential justified them. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0025] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0076] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0133]

- **Founder commitment:** In the cited framework, Hustle Fund would not fund a part-time founder. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0077]

- **Integrity and efficiency:** Misrepresentation is an automatic rejection, as is extremely high burn with little achieved. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0072] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0073]

- **Decision mechanics:** Companies are scored across multiple axes, but no minimum overall score mechanically determines investment; a champion model permits individual investment professionals to back contentious outliers. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0099] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0101] [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0102]

- **Personal angel carve-out:** Yin separately reports personal investments in non-software D2C, food and beverage, fashion, and other non-tech categories. These personal activities must not be folded into Hustle Fund’s software mandate. [ev:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0018] [ctx:elizabeth-yin-hustle-fund-0038]
