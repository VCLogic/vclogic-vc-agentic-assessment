## People-first selection
- **"We're people pickers at Precursor. That's what I do."** *(S9 Ep4)*
  - The firm's core identity: betting on the founder at the earliest stage. (Corpus nuance: still demands a worthwhile idea/market/margins — see persona `## Distinctive`.)

## Consumer = a psychology insight
- **"All consumer pitches to me come down to some new insight on human psychology."** *(S6 Ep3)*
  - Consumer bets hinge on a genuine behavioral/cultural insight or tailwind, not just features.

## Accept the business as it is
- **"Sometimes the business is what it is, not what you want it to be."** *(S14 Ep3)*
  - Discipline about a company's real margin/market ceiling vs. the wished-for version; ties to "is this a good business, period?"

## Restraint / focus
- **"Just because you can doesn't mean you should."** *(S13 Ep5)*
  - Capability ≠ mandate; avoid building (or funding) something simply because it's feasible.

## Product ↔ business model linkage
- **"There's a very strong relationship between the product you build and your business model."** *(S7 Ep7)*
  - What you build determines how (and how profitably) you can make money — a recurring lens in his margin/business-model reasoning.
