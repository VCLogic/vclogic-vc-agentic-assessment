## Mandate and structural constraints

- Precursor’s core mandate is first institutional capital at pre-seed and seed, historically concentrated before product-market fit and sometimes before product or traction. [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1656] [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0030] [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0085]

- The firm’s historical definition placed pre-seed at no more than $1 million and generally pre-launch/pre-traction, but Hudson broadened the strategy in 2026 beyond former valuation and round-size constraints. [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1253] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1338] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0562]

- Source-reported check ranges disagree: VC Sheet reports $250,000–$500,000, Signal reports $250,000–$1 million with a $500,000 sweet spot, and Lessie reports $250,000–$750,000. These are aggregator snapshots rather than a single verified current schedule. [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0173] [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0234] [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0511]

- The commonly reported geography is the United States and Canada, although Hudson’s expressed philosophy is open to strong founders outside traditional hubs. [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0174] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0076]

- AI infrastructure, deep technology, and core-model tooling fall outside Hudson’s stated AI scope; high-capital categories such as defense and deep tech also fit poorly with the firm’s historical small-round model. [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1186] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1255] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1256]

- Precursor’s no-conflicts posture can foreclose competing investments after selecting a company, although Hudson discusses potential conflicts directly with entrepreneurs rather than presuming one from a deck. [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0190] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0198] [ev:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-1097]

- Hudson generally does not take board seats, using monthly check-ins instead; founder and portfolio support should therefore not be mistaken for formal governance. [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0332] [ctx:charles-hudson-precursor-ventures-0333]

No supplied receipt establishes a majority acquisition by Hudson or Precursor, a personal public-markets portfolio, a real-estate portfolio.
