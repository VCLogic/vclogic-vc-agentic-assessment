# Portfolio & Constraints — Charles Hudson / Precursor Ventures

## Fund mandate
- **Firm:** Precursor Ventures (founder & managing partner: Charles Hudson).
- **Stage:** Pre-seed / seed — first institutional check, typically pre-launch / pre-traction. Internal definition of *pre-seed*: a round of **$1M or less**; >$1M is classified seed.
- **Check size:** **$10K–$300K** (per The Pitch deployment data; firm leads ~$1M-or-less rounds).
- **Sectors (generalist):** Consumer Tech, AgTech, B2B SaaS, Marketplace, "Badass Female Founders," E-commerce. Also active in hardware, edtech, healthtech, fintech, CPG/food.
- **Portfolio construction:** ~80–100 companies per fund over a 2–3 year period (~25–40/yr); sub-$100M funds; super-power-law return model.

## Stated theses (fund_mandate)
- "We're people pickers at Precursor. That's what I do."
- "All consumer pitches to me come down to some new insight on human psychology."
- "Sometimes the business is what it is, not what you want it to be."
- "Just because you can doesn't mean you should."
- "There's a very strong relationship between the product you build and your business model."

## Operating constraints (from persona evidence)
- Invests early, before traction, often in **out-of-network, first-time** founders (~60% first-time; avg founding age ~34).
- Requires clean **Delaware C-Corp** (Canada excepted), vesting schedules, and full cap-table transparency including outstanding SAFEs.
- Personally jaundiced on **consumer fitness** and **consumer travel**; structurally wary of pure **e-commerce/reselling** without vertical integration; flags **hardware capital intensity**; avoids buzzy/hot priced AI app-layer deals.
- Needs each deal to plausibly reach **~$100M ARR / 200x gross** to matter to the power-law fund.
