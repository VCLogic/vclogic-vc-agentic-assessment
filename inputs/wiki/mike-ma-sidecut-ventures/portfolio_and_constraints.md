## Mandate and operating constraints

- Sidecut is reported as a pre-seed fund seeking coachable, mission-driven founders; repeated filters are product, traction, positive impact, and a company value below $10 million. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0338] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0622]

- The formal impact scope is variously stated as economic mobility, education, healthcare, climate, and disability/accessibility, with some sources naming only a subset and other remarks exploring justice and democracy technology. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0359] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0131]

- Sidecut generally contemplates approximately $200,000–$300,000 checks; other sources report $250,000 and $250,000–$500,000, so sizing should be treated as source-date dependent. [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0240] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0339] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0027]

- The firm can lead or follow, has reportedly led six rounds, and has participated in follow-ons through some pro rata rights; the sources do not establish a universal lead, ownership, or reserve target. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0160] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0262] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0266]

- The 30-day pre-investment working period is a substantive selection mechanism testing founder execution, coachability, mutual fit, and Sidecut’s ability to add value. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0631] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0285]

- Sidecut’s principal value-add is sales, marketing, product-led growth, go-to-market, and traction support, tailored to the company rather than delivered through a uniform program. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0197] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0198]

- Price discipline centers on single-digit-million entry values, with roughly $6 million cited as a median in one source and below $10 million as the repeated cap. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0289] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0372]

- Ma reports that Sidecut receives information and board-observer rights when it is a major investor; another account says many unconverted instruments carry information but no voting rights. No general board-seat policy is established. [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0265] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0065]

- Portfolio construction was described as roughly ten startup investments per year, while Ma says a portfolio approaching 80 deals would impair returns. [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0096] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0214]

- Warm introductions are not required: both warm and cold opportunities reportedly use the same published intake process, and every submitted application is reviewed. [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0133] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0273]

- Explicit competence boundaries include horizontal LLMs and some technical categories where Ma lacks an informational edge; some remarks also flag limited hardware/CPG expertise, although reported hardware investing shows that this is a diligence constraint rather than an absolute ban. [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0649] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0468] [ev:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0110] [ctx:mike-ma-sidecut-ventures-0267]

No evidence in the packet supports attributing majority acquisitions, majority-controlled operating companies, a public-markets portfolio, or a real-estate portfolio to Ma or Sidecut. Named employers, accelerators, advisers, co-investors, mentors, media projects, examples, competitors, missed deals, and referral targets should remain outside the holdings list unless a cited relationship explicitly says otherwise.
