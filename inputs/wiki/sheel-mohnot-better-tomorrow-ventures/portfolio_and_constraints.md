## Mandate and structural constraints

- BTV generally leads pre-seed and seed fintech rounds; later sources add vertical AI, and the official site reports checks of $500,000–$4 million. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0648] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1090]

- The broad fintech definition covers infrastructure, vertical SaaS, marketplaces, and businesses expected to derive substantial revenue from financial services. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1124] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1687]

- Category conflicts are material: BTV generally backs one company per category and avoids competitors, including among accelerator participants. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0045] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0046]

- Ownership matters because BTV provides intensive support. Sources report targets around 10–15% and selective rather than automatic follow-ons. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0490] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0492]

- The opportunity fund primarily makes later-stage follow-ons in existing portfolio companies and requires at least 5x potential upside. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0606] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1453]

- Geography is formally global but operationally constrained by local knowledge, follow-on financing, and BTV’s ability to add differentiated value. One later source reports a roughly 70% US portfolio after an earlier approximately one-third emerging-market allocation. [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0335] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0142]

- Direct lenders, generic merchant processing, undifferentiated consumer fintech, standalone crypto, thin AI wrappers, and harmful-to-consumer products face substantial skepticism, but several are selective aversions rather than universal prohibitions. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0601] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0683] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1420]
