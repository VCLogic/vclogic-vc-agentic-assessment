# Sheel Mohnot — Recurring Investment Theses

- **Direct venture — broad fintech:** Financial services are enormous, inherently digital, still under-digitized, and filled with manual workflows and customer pain; BTV therefore treats fintech as a broad collection of markets. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1500] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1503] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0953]

- **Direct venture — infrastructure and embedded finance:** He repeatedly seeks APIs and enabling layers for banking, lending, trading, payroll, accounting, insurance, and compliance, especially where vertical software or marketplaces already possess distribution and data. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0009] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0306] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1361]

- **Direct venture — vertical SaaS monetized by finance:** He expects many vertical SaaS companies to earn more from payments and other embedded financial products than from core subscriptions, broadening BTV beyond companies conventionally labeled fintech. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0347] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1686] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1823]

- **Direct venture — services-as-software / vertical AI:** Later evidence shows strong interest in AI businesses that sell completed outcomes, replace tedious labor, and exploit workflows or compliance that generic models cannot serve. Accounting recurs because labor shortages and rejected customer demand validate willingness to buy automation. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1025] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1026] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1664]

- **Evolution / tension — AI:** He prefers AI companies because observed growth has been faster, but distrusts AI-washing, thin wrappers, weak retention, foundation-model cannibalization, and extreme revenue multiples. AI is an enabler, not a substitute for a customer problem or defensibility. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0163] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0181] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0438] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1449]

- **Direct venture — enterprise and B2B tilt:** BTV has leaned enterprise, infrastructure, and B2B because consumer acquisition is difficult, features converge, and competitors fight for the primary relationship. New consumer bets need genuine uniqueness, trust, and differentiated distribution. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0473] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0267] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1506]

- **Evolution / exception — consumer:** He remained cautious after earlier broad consumer opportunities were captured, but later called the current period more attractive for consumer investing and expressed growing interest as AI improves advertising. This is renewed selectivity, not a wholesale reversal. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1374] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1039] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1692]

- **Direct venture — trusted autonomous finance:** He favors products automating beneficial financial actions over dashboards or education requiring continual user attention, provided the product earns trust and preserves visibility or approval. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0553] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0555] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0693]

- **Direct venture — lending ecosystem, usually not direct lending:** He likes underwriting, servicing, distribution, and credit infrastructure but generally avoids direct lenders because growth can conceal losses and repeated equity may be required to support debt. Embedded distribution and proprietary data are favorable exceptions. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0601] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0602] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0976]

- **Direct venture — insurance:** Insurance is historically his most-invested fintech category. He favors better consumer products, direct distribution, superior data, genuinely new underwriting, and embedded coverage; he is negative on renter’s-insurance scale, undifferentiated digitization, and capital-heavy carriers. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1277] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1613] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1604] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1598]

- **Direct venture — global and emerging markets:** His longstanding emerging-market interest favors weak legacy infrastructure, expanding smartphone access, large domestic markets or credible regional expansion, locally native models, and local co-investors. Later strategy shifted toward the United States as frontier-market financing and liquidity became harder. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0257] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0409] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0506] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1435]

- **Direct venture — productive inclusion:** He prefers credit that expands productive capacity rather than consumption and favors inclusion paired with strong profit potential. BTV rejects products expected to worsen customers’ financial lives. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0403] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1319] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1421]

- **Direct venture — crypto only with practical utility:** Standalone crypto is outside the core mandate. Cross-border stablecoins, payments, or credit infrastructure may qualify when a practical fintech use case exists; most blockchain pitches and ICOs fail his necessity and economics tests. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0227] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0804] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0808]

- **Direct venture — founder-led opportunism:** Within fintech he resists overly narrow sub-theses and often backs exceptional founders able to adapt. Theme enthusiasm must not override founder quality. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0304] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0111] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1090]

- **Fund allocation — early-stage concentration:** BTV usually leads pre-seed and seed, seeks meaningful ownership, avoids category conflicts, reserves capital, and concentrates later capital in roughly its strongest 20% rather than automatically supporting every company. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0516] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0487] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1730]

- **Fund allocation — power law and fund size:** Mohnot believes a few outliers drive returns, very large funds weaken valuation discipline, and fund size should derive from portfolio count, check size, ownership, and reserves rather than AUM maximization. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0234] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0747] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0749]

- **Fund allocation — liquidity:** Experience made him more willing to sell some secondary holdings when markets are favorable, ideally returning the fund while retaining upside. This coexists with skepticism toward premature exits too small to affect fund returns. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0109] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0127] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0730]

- **Public markets:** These views are distinct from BTV’s seed mandate. He reported buying NVIDIA in 2017 and later praised its integrated hardware, networking, and software moat; he also disclosed a contemporaneous short position in Lemonade. [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0229] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1255] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0858]

- **Public/private late-stage AI:** He later said he would probably buy OpenAI and Anthropic at the discussed prices while stressing uncertain growth, revenue quality, competition, capital intensity, and the need for application-layer economics. This is not evidence of a holding. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1238] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1241] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1291]

- **Real estate analysis:** He expressed interest in real estate beyond strict fintech and evaluated a proposed property business through utilization, comparables, operating rules, regulatory risk, and downside asset value. This was opportunity analysis, not a reported completed property investment. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0013] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0252] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0254]

- **Operating-company strategy:** His founder experience favors customer discovery, rapid experimentation, disciplined acquisition, focused pivots, and building the strongest independent business rather than optimizing for acquisition. Applied to selection, these are inferred unless separately stated as investment criteria. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0449] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0451] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1894]
