# Sheel Mohnot — Investment Memory

This memory resolves to Sheel Mohnot, co-founder and general partner of Better Tomorrow Ventures (BTV), excluding same-name people. It distinguishes source-linked investor statements with automated attribution from source-reported context and separates BTV, 500 FinTech, personal, public-market, crypto, founder, adviser, board, and LP activity where the packet permits. Many sources are undated, duplicated, imperfectly transcribed, or missing transcripts; current-sounding positions are therefore only as current as their source, and the record is neither exhaustive nor independently verified.

## founder_team

Mohnot’s earliest-stage underwriting is founder-first: products, technologies, and customer segments may change, while the founders remain. He believes a strong team can adapt out of a weak initial market, whereas a weak team may waste an attractive one. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0634] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1780] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1879]

- **In:** founders who move urgently, ship and iterate rapidly, learn from customers, and show observable progress between meetings. Speed and tenacity are his most consistently repeated founder criteria. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0739] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1081] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1732]

- **In:** resourceful people who overcome difficult constraints, persist through setbacks, and pivot when evidence invalidates the initial approach. Several of his strongest reported portfolio companies pivoted, reinforcing—but not proving—the value he assigns to adaptability. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0457] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0876] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0455]

- **In:** CEOs who articulate a compelling future clearly enough to recruit employees, persuade customers, and attract investors. At pre-seed he prefers candid conversation and concrete experiments over presentations implying false certainty. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0758] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0735] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1671]

- **In:** intellectually curious founders with deep market command who can teach him, respond thoughtfully to challenge, and revise beliefs when facts change. Reasoned disagreement is acceptable; refusal to learn is not. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0873] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1356] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1346]

- **In:** relevant fintech or industry experience where regulatory or operational mistakes are consequential. Prior work may supply relationships and trust, but bank employment is not required and can sometimes be a negative rather than positive signal. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0725] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0787] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0789]

- **In:** exceptional recruiting ability and the potential to lead a large organization; he has called recruiting potentially the most important founder skill because later hires determine what the company can build. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1342] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1651]

- **Selective preference:** two or three complementary co-founders are his usual preference, although one to three is acceptable and he is comfortable backing solo founders. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1533] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1534] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1121]

- **Out / strong negatives:** dishonesty, arrogance, weak urgency, unwillingness to work hard, failure to build or attempt sales, and obscuring direct revenue with proxy metrics. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0048] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0050] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0657] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1490]

- **Diligence boundary:** a prior fraud made him more alert to inconsistencies and more reference-intensive. Inability to obtain references from a founder’s former company can stop an investment, although mixed references are not automatically disqualifying when the risk is understood. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0728] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0729] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0767]

- **Inferred question:** “What did you build, sell, learn, or change since our last conversation, and what hard obstacle did you overcome?” This translates his emphasis on speed, customer learning, and tenacity; it is not a quoted mandatory script. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0472] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0600]

## market_opportunity

He wants businesses capable of becoming very large and substantially profitable. Sources express the necessary outcome variously as billion-dollar-plus, approximately $5 billion, or approximately $10 billion; those figures reflect changing fund-return contexts, not a universal revenue gate. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0316] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1116] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1577]

- **In:** large, growing markets where success can yield durable cash flows; he sometimes gives market growth more weight than current absolute size. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0479] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0856]

- **Core opportunity:** financial services are exceptionally large, naturally digital, still under-digitized, and burdened by manual processes and poor customer experiences. This supports a broad fintech mandate rather than a narrow product category. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1500] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1501] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1665]

- **In:** underserved customers and broken workflows, including small-business finance, cross-border payments, accounting labor, private-company liquidity, employee option exercise, and home buying. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0317] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0282] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0713] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0715]

- **Global preference with qualification:** he historically described a roughly two-thirds-developed/one-third-emerging allocation but later reported greater US concentration. In emerging markets he seeks very large reachable populations, expanding middle classes or smartphone penetration, and paths into adjacent markets; small country-only companies face scale and financing constraints. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0372] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0607] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0980] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0769]

- **Out:** opportunities that cannot matter to the fund even if they become sound standalone businesses. An excellent founder outcome can still be too small for institutional venture economics. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1052] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1173]

- **Inferred questions:** “Who is the initial customer, why is that population underserved, and how does the wedge expand into a much larger market?” His Robinhood and Chime retrospectives emphasize that he initially misunderstood their actual customer wedges. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0963] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0967]

## competition_defensibility

Mohnot does not require contrarian positioning. He will invest in popular categories when he believes BTV can identify and win the right company; selecting and accessing a likely category leader matters more than being non-consensus for its own sake. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0171] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0173]

- **In:** workflow integration, differentiated distribution, proprietary data, network effects, customer trust, operational complexity, and superior execution. He increasingly describes defensibility as moving from purely technical moats toward customer-acquisition and product moats. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0034] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1303] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1057]

- **In:** vertical AI with specialized terminology, compliance, workflow integrations, or proprietary behavioral data that generic models cannot readily replace. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0242] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0243] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0660]

- **Out / caution:** thin AI wrappers, standalone writing tools, generic consumer AI, and applications vulnerable to foundation-model expansion. Cannibalization by model providers is a central diligence concern. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0240] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0241] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0651]

- **Out / caution:** categories containing many well-funded lookalikes pursuing the same buyers. He believes this damages pricing, profitability, and each company’s probability of success; BTV has skipped categories when too many similar companies appeared. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0099] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1202] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1710]

- **Exception:** apparent technical simplicity is not sufficient grounds to reject a company. His Patreon miss taught him that simple payment functionality can conceal product, community, or distribution value. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1408]

- **Portfolio boundary:** BTV generally backs one company per category and avoids competitors, prioritizing obligations to existing founders. Later conflicts may be situational, but even small exploratory checks can foreclose the eventual winner. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0045] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0047] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0118]

## product_solution

A real, painful customer problem comes before the technology label. He looks for direct customer understanding, concrete current use cases, and products that make finance easier or automate work customers cannot economically perform manually. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0061] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0429] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0638]

- **In:** infrastructure enabling banking, lending, trading, payroll, accounting, insurance, compliance, or other financial capabilities inside vertical SaaS, marketplaces, and non-fintech distribution channels. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0009] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0305] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0678]

- **In:** services-as-software, especially AI that replaces tedious labor and sells a completed outcome rather than merely a software tool. Accounting, receivables, mortgages, loan onboarding, and professional services recur as examples. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0177] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1026] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1513]

- **In:** trusted consumer-finance automation that takes beneficial action with user visibility or approval. He finds this more compelling than dashboards or education requiring continuous attention. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0554] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0693] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0694]

- **Operating advice; inferred selection preference:** trust, security, interface quality, and accuracy are unusually consequential in financial services. He prefers a slower launch that establishes trust over speed that risks damaging errors. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0161] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0162] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0665]

- **Out:** blockchain or crypto inserted without necessity or a compelling present-day use case. Standalone crypto is excluded, but fintech intersections such as cross-border stablecoin access may qualify when they deliver tangible utility. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0017] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0227] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0482]

- **Inferred question:** “What becomes materially better for the end user, and why does this require your product rather than a generic model, incumbent feature, or simpler workflow?” [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1009] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1010]

## traction_growth

At pre-seed and seed he does not impose a fixed revenue checklist. In one source he says he no longer invests from decks alone and requires a built product rather than a presentation alone, although it need not be polished; acceptable progress can include customer discovery, partnerships, regulatory work, acquisition experiments, audience development, or other fintech-appropriate evidence. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0658] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1326] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1574]

- **Strong validation, not a universal prerequisite:** upfront or continuing payment is stronger evidence than free interest. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0446] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0889] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1484]

- **In:** trajectory and visible inflection, repeatable usage, organic referrals, and evidence customers would miss the product. At later seed he expects more proof but still avoids a fixed numeric threshold. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1125] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0445] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0741]

- **Discount:** waitlists, promotional sharing, trials booked as ARR, nonrecurring revenue, and extraordinary growth whose retention or durability is uncertain. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0136] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0287] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0955]

- **AI-specific scrutiny:** fast AI growth is attractive, but he questions retention, model-provider displacement, and revenue persistence. An AI identity is neither necessary nor sufficient. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0163] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0060] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0182]

## business_model_economics

The ultimate test is a credible path to substantial long-term profit, not financing velocity or headline growth. He examines scaled unit economics and the composition and quality of revenue rather than accepting reported totals at face value. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0476] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0063] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1239]

- **Preferred revenue quality:** direct customer revenue first and payments next; elsewhere he ranks SaaS and payment processing highest, API/AUM/interchange next, and PFOF, lead generation, transaction fees, interest income, and spread revenue lower. These are relative preferences, not absolute exclusions. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0040] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0881]

- **In:** vertical SaaS and B2B marketplaces where payments, lending, payroll, insurance, or banking can expand revenue beyond subscription fees; proximity to transactions improves monetization. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0029] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0307] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1089]

- **In:** capital-efficient technology businesses whose growth does not require proportional hiring or continual equity-funded customer acquisition. Falling inference costs may improve AI margins, but he does not assume AI reduces total capital needs. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0179] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0180] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1056]

- **Lending caution:** he repeatedly avoids direct lenders because originations can manufacture apparent growth, poor underwriting stays hidden, money is commoditized, and expanding debt capacity often requires recurring equity. This is a strong aversion, not a blanket prohibition: embedded distribution and proprietary underwriting data can strengthen the case. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0203] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0602] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0976]

- **Consumer economics:** he has cited sub-one-year payback and long customer lives as attractive and criticized two- or three-year paybacks. Dependence on paid acquisition without brand or differentiated distribution is a major concern. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0211] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1041] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1287]

## deal_terms_valuation

Mohnot is price-sensitive because seed entry price must preserve outlier return potential. He maintains an internal fair-price view and will not match bids two or three times above it merely to win. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0139] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0495]

- **Historical ranges, not timeless caps:** many 500 FinTech entries were below $5 million; earlier BTV sources describe traditional seed broadly below $15–20 million; later commentary cites approximately $5–12 million pre-seed and $12–25 million seed. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0515] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0517] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0212] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0213]

- **Price boundary:** he has repeatedly called $20–25 million seed pricing too high for adequate fund returns, while acknowledging that exceptional or proven founders can justify somewhat higher prices and a different risk proposition. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1268] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1767] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0172]

- **Check and ownership:** reported initial checks vary by vehicle and vintage from roughly $500,000 to $4 million, with earlier averages around $1–2 million; BTV usually leads and has targeted roughly 10–15% ownership. [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0060] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0383] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0490]

- **Structural boundary:** one source states a minimum 10% ownership requirement, while another says insufficient ownership makes intensive support uneconomic. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1579] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1454]

- **Instrument flexibility:** he is generally indifferent between priced equity and convertible financing and accepts YC SAFEs; successive SAFEs at different prices can be reasonable when company circumstances genuinely changed. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1274] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0468]

- **Out:** badly distorted cap tables, including an incubator owning roughly 40%, extreme co-founder inequality, or a part-time adviser owning more equity than the CEO. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1107] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1110] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1111]

## timing

His timing framework is cyclical. He characterized 2021 fintech enthusiasm as excessive and the 2022–2023 retreat as an excessive correction; later material describes fintech as favorable again, supported by acquisitions, IPOs, public performance, and strong follow-on financings. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1204] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0384] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0186]

- **Later positive themes:** fintech infrastructure, embedded finance, vertical AI, AI accounting, services-as-software, software for financial advisers, and renewed selective consumer opportunity as AI improves advertising. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0166] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0677] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1691] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1692]

- **Evolution:** embedded finance developed more slowly than BTV expected, but reported attach rates above 50% among some new customers renewed support for the thesis. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0349] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0350]

- **AI tension:** AI adoption is an immediate competitive necessity, but broad labor displacement and measurable macroeconomic effects appear later than early forecasts; physical AI remains less mature and more capital intensive. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0160] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0133] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0580]

- **Rate sensitivity:** higher rates hurt growth-first and credit-spread companies while benefiting deposit-rich models; frozen public markets delay exits and weaken later-stage return timing. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0311] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1772] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0213]

## investor_fit_constraints

BTV’s center of gravity is global pre-seed and seed fintech, usually leading a company’s first institutional round and remaining highly engaged. Later descriptions explicitly add vertical AI, while older sources call the mandate exclusively fintech; the tension is partly reconciled by BTV’s broad “everything is fintech” framing. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0303] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0648] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0023]

- **Best fit:** founders seeking an active operator-investor helping with hiring, distribution, partnerships, culture, bank relationships, and later fundraising. Communication may be daily early and taper as the company matures. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0770] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1140] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1300]

- **Geography:** formally global, but practical knowledge and local support matter. BTV often invests internationally beside a local firm or angel; unfamiliarity may require later-stage evidence or cause a pass, and the portfolio has shifted toward the United States. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0138] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0981] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1435]

- **Team location:** he prefers in-person and co-located teams but does not make proximity absolute; he has completed a deal without meeting the founder and believes remote investors can add hiring and fundraising value. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0090] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0207] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0137]

- **Mission boundary:** shareholder returns are primary, but he favors financial inclusion and productive activity, and BTV automatically rejects prospects it believes will worsen customers’ financial lives. He may personally support desired products that fail fund-scale economics. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1318] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0403] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1421] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0318]

- **Process:** there is no fixed meeting count or elapsed-time rule; conviction can be rapid or take months. Diligence scales with check size and may cover customers, founder references, market structure, regulation, partnerships, and terms. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0054] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0055] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0470]

- **Tentative inferred checklist:** Is the team honest, fast, tenacious, knowledgeable, recruitable, and coachable? Is the problem painful and validated? Is the initial customer wedge correctly understood? Can the market and economics support an outlier? Is there differentiated distribution, data, workflow, trust, or execution? Are traction and revenue durable? Are valuation, ownership, dilution, and conflicts acceptable? This consolidates recurring evidence and is not a formal published scorecard. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0986] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1751] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1830]

## Distinctive / doesn't-fit-the-taxonomy

- Mohnot’s investment identity developed through founder experience, personal angel investing, self-financing early fund investments, and specialization. He personally funded the first stretch of 500 FinTech before outside capital arrived; these are reported biographical facts, not proof of why he holds particular investment preferences. [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0232] [ctx:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0233]

- He is not doctrinaire about consensus: he has succeeded in overlooked companies but also argues that power-law returns justify fighting to win highly demanded category leaders. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0461] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0462]

- He explicitly recognizes his own misses and biases: Robinhood and Chime taught him to identify the real new customer population; Honey taught humility about scale; Patreon taught him not to equate technical simplicity with weak opportunity; and prior failure should not mechanically disqualify a different founder. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0967] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0197] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1408] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1177]

- Fund construction materially shapes selection: venture returns are power-law distributed, so BTV reserves follow-on capital and concentrates it in emerging winners rather than following every company automatically. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0484] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1231] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-1730]

- His operating advice should not be overread as universal investment law. Product focus, founder-origin problems, customer discovery, rapid iteration, and disciplined cuts illuminate what he admires, but applying them to investment selection is inferred unless he expressly identifies them as criteria. [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0449] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0451] [ev:sheel-mohnot-better-tomorrow-ventures-0990]
