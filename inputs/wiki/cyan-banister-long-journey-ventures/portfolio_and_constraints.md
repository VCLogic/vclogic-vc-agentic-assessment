## Mandate and operating constraints

- **Personal stage boundary:** Banister expressly specializes in early-stage investing and says later-stage investing does not fit her. [ev:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0004] [ev:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0005]

- **Firm stage:** Long Journey is described by its own page and outside profiles as early-stage; third-party directories place activity at pre-seed, seed, and Series A. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0085] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0259]

- **Sector mandate:** Firm commentary presents Long Journey as intentionally generalist, even though directories list AI, fintech, health, deeptech/hardware, and SaaS and recent reporting highlights nanotech and biotech. The sector lists should not override the firm’s own generalist statement. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0223] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0187] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0042]

- **Check size:** Third-party sources report $100,000–$1 million average checks, while another reports $25,000–$1 million and an older model page reports $400,000–$700,000 for Fund I. These are unverified, potentially period-specific ranges rather than firm commitments. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0188] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0178] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0229]

- **Leading and syndication:** A 2020 episode outline says Banister favors party rounds, while VC Sheet says Long Journey can lead. Both structures may therefore be possible, subject to confirmation. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0032] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0261]

- **Fund capacity:** A secondary report places Long Journey IV at approximately $181.8 million. Lee Jacobs says it doubled firm AUM, but the announcement literally prints ‘$181,818,181.8 million,’ an apparent unit typo rather than a reliable exact amount. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0076] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0098]

- **Concentration:** Banister historically made an extreme personal SpaceX bet, but her Substrate account says legal constraints prevented putting an entire fund into one company. Personal concentration must not be treated as an institutional mandate. [ev:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0021] [ev:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0008] [ev:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0009]

- **Shared household investing:** Banister says she and Scott Banister treat their assets and investing partnership as shared. This complicates efforts to allocate older angel holdings to one spouse, and later portfolio lists often explicitly describe a joint portfolio. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0212] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0213] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0008]

- **Value-add posture:** Long Journey describes direct but caring founder support, while a third-party model attributes fundraising, product/growth, and recruiting assistance to the firm. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0126] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0230]

- **No supported LP allocation ledger:** The materials identify Banister as a GP and investor but do not name outside funds in which she is an LP; Founders Fund and Long Journey are operating affiliations, not evidence of passive LP exposure. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0084] [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0086]

- **No supported board-role ledger:** Banister discusses how she behaves in boardrooms, but the supplied receipts do not identify a specific company board seat; no board membership should be inferred. [ctx:cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures-0118]
