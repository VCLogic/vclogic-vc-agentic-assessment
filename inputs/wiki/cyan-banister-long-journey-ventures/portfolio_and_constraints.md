# Portfolio & Constraints — Cyan Banister / Long Journey Ventures

## Fund mandate

- **Firm:** Long Journey Ventures.
- **Stage:** Early-stage / seed, with Cyan describing her lane as first-check investing.
- **Observed check range:** **$25K–$1M**.
- **Published categories:** B2B SaaS, CPG, fashion, edtech, and investments in female founders; the broader policy is generalist and organized around “magically weird” people and ideas.
- **Return model:** Power-law venture investing; each investment must have credible fund-returning upside.
- **Conflict policy:** Does not invest in direct competitors.

## Stated theses

- “I try to look six years out and think about what's coming next.”
- “When I have high conviction, I'm in.”
- The founder should know something others do not: an insight, moat, or intelligence that reveals a non-obvious opportunity.
- A life-long inception story signals mission-level founder motivation.

## Operating constraints distilled from persona evidence

- Invests at the raw, pre-product-market-fit stage and can back founders without a track record.
- Requires massive outcomes and a plausible power-law return.
- Avoids direct portfolio competitors.
- Rejects deadlocked founder structures and investigates debt, ownership, and unexplained cap-table stakes.
- Avoids crowded application-layer hype cycles where differentiation and winner selection are unclear.
- Recognizes when another investor has materially better hardware, manufacturing, distribution, or cost expertise.
