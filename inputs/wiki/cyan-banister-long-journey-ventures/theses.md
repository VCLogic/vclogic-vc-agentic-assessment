# Investment Theses — Cyan Banister / Long Journey Ventures

Source: `data/investors/cyan-banister-long-journey-ventures.json` → `investment_theses` (quotes retained; episode references are provenance metadata only, and no Pitch transcript was read).

## Look beyond the present

- **“I try to look six years out and think about what's coming next.”** *(S15 Ep3)*
  - The investable opportunity is often a future behavior or category that is still uncomfortable or non-obvious today.

## High conviction before product-market fit

- **“When I have high conviction, I'm in. That's what they [founders] want—someone to believe them. I'm comfortable with a company that doesn't have product market fit and hasn't figured it out yet.”** *(S15 Ep3)*
  - First-check investing underwrites the founder and insight before conventional proof exists.

## Non-consensus knowledge

- **“It's whether they know something that nobody else seems to know. Do they have an insight or a moat or some sort of intelligence that they're onto something.”** *(S14; episode reference unspecified in source)*
  - Weirdness must contain a real insight, not novelty for its own sake.

## Life-mission inception stories

- **“I'm always drawn to an inception story that starts from age seven. Because that means it's their life mission. I love entrepreneurs like this and I would back them all day long.”** *(S15 Ep3)*
  - Long-running personal motivation is evidence of authenticity and staying power.
